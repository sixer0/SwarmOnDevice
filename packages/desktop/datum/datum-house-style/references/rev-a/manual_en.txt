 C H A P T E R 1

 Using this manual
 This manual gives GSP one way to write every English document, so that two writers, human or AI,
 produce the same form, language, and evidence.
  It rests on DATUM v1.9 Rev O and its tokens.json, beside the Indonesian edition (1.7). As the machine
 specification for human and AI writers, it names tools, scripts, file paths, and its edition history, which
 DT-42c and EN-L4-03 forbid in documents for readers.

 1.1   The governing rule
 Put on the page only evidence and the apparatus that proves it. Decoration is     DT-01a                                                                                       MANDATORY
 available to anyone, apparatus only to the firm that did the engineering.                  TEST · EYE  Every element can be
                                                                                                                                                 pointed to as evidence or apparatus

 Apparatus lets a reader check a claim: source lines, provenance marks, derivation blocks, requirement
 IDs, and the Evidence Rail (Chapter 7). Right: an Indonesian Navy (TNI AL) perimeter study gives the
 detection range with ◇ VENDOR , the source grade, and the datasheet. Wrong: the same figure in a coloured
 box, unsourced.

 1.2   Rule levels and codes

 9.5   Captions, SOURCE lines, and the home of numbers
 A caption is one sentence above the chart (CH-05). The SOURCE line gives base document, date, and
 provenance mark (DT-15), and values come from the register (DT-23).
 An English caption opens with its subject and a finite verb, never "This chart shows". It gives a count with
 its total, not a bare percentage: "Of four arrays, one is above 80 % fill". The date closes it: "as of 30
 September 2026" for a snapshot, "January to June 2026" for a period.
 Axis text follows Chapter 6. The unit appears once, in the axis title ("Power draw (W)"), and ticks read
 1,000, 0.5, and −1.2 with a true minus. Money axes put "IDR billion" in the title, never 5M or 5bn on ticks.
 Time ticks read Jan, Feb, Mar, with the year once.
 A chart's data may also appear in an annex table (CH-15), and prose quotes at most one chart value, with
 no mark, because the chart is its home. Right: "Only West Post is over budget." Wrong: prose and a table
 repeating all five values.
